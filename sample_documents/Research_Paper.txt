AUTONOMOUS AGENT ORCHESTRATION FOR UNSTRUCTURED DOCUMENT TAXONOMY GENERATION

Abstract:
Managing large volumes of unstructured digital documents presents significant challenges in enterprise knowledge engineering. This paper introduces DocuMind, a novel multi-stage AI agent framework for automated document perception, semantic classification, and taxonomy generation. By coupling modular prompt flow orchestration with structured JSON schemas, our approach achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in multi-label categorisation and path routing.

1. INTRODUCTION
Unstructured files such as academic publications, financial reports, and technical documentation account for over 80% of digital enterprise data. Traditional keyword indexing systems suffer from semantic ambiguity. Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) enable contextual reasoning over text corpora.

2. METHODOLOGY AND AGENT ARCHITECTURE
Our system decomposes document organization into four primary agent reasoning primitives:
1. Perception Stage: Extracts clean text streams and structural metadata from PDF, DOCX, and plain text formats.
2. Reasoning Stage: Evaluates contextual embeddings against dynamic multi-domain taxonomies.
3. Decision Stage: Emits validated JSON payloads containing category, sub-category, tags, and suggested directory paths.
4. Human Approval Stage: Presents actionable recommendations to human supervisors before disk mutations occur.

3. EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS AND EVALUATION
We evaluated the framework on a benchmark dataset of 500 heterogeneous documents across Education, Career, Research, Finance, and Personal domains. The agent achieved an overall classification accuracy of 94.2% with a mean inference latency under 1.8 seconds.
